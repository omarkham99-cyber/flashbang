Flashbang Privacy Policy

Effective September 23, 2026.

Flashbang is published by O10 Media LLC. It collects no data.

  • It has no accounts, analytics, ads or tracking.
  • It makes no network connections.
  • Sounds you add or record are kept in the app on your phone and never leave it. Deleting a sound or the app removes them.
  • The microphone is used only while you're recording a sound.
  • It turns on the flashlight but never uses the camera to take pictures or video.

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page.
